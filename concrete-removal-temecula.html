How We Approach Concrete Removal

Standard residential slabs break down efficiently with the right equipment. Heavier or reinforced concrete gets factored into the quote up front.

Full Recycling

Every load goes to a licensed recycling facility, not a landfill. Disposal is included in the quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does concrete removal cost in Temecula?

Concrete removal in Temecula typically runs $2-$6 per square foot depending on thickness, reinforcement, access, and project size. Standard residential driveways run $2,500-$4,000 including breaking, loading, hauling, and disposal. Smaller projects start around $800.

How long does concrete removal take?

Most projects take 1-2 days. A standard driveway is typically finished in one working day including breaking, loading, hauling, and cleanup. Larger or heavily reinforced projects may need 2 days.

What equipment do you use?

Skid steers with hydraulic breaker attachments, hand-operated jackhammers for precision work, and heavy-duty trailers for hauling. Equipment is matched to access and scope, not applied one-size-fits-all.

Do I need a permit for concrete removal in Temecula?

Removal-only projects typically don't require permits. If you're replacing the concrete, expanding the footprint, or affecting drainage, permits may be needed from Temecula Building & Safety. We flag this during the site evaluation.

What do you do with the broken concrete?

All concrete is hauled to licensed recycling facilities where it's crushed into aggregate for road base and new construction. Disposal fees are included in our quotes.

Can you remove concrete with rebar?

Yes, we handle reinforced concrete including rebar and wire mesh. Reinforcement adds cutting time, which factors into the quote.

Can you remove concrete in tight spaces?

Yes, we handle tight-access removal throughout Temecula using hand tools and smaller machines where larger equipment won't fit. Tight access adds labor time and typically costs more per square foot.
