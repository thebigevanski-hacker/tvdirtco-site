Common Reasons for Driveway Removal

Complete Replacement

Old driveways crack and settle over time. After 20-30 years, repairs stop being cost-effective and a fresh pour with modern base prep and drainage makes more sense.

Garage Expansion

Adding a third bay or extending garage depth means removing the approach section first so contractors can pour a seamless extension.

Landscape Redesign

Converting oversized driveways to drought-friendly landscaping or permeable pavers starts with removing the unwanted section.

Drainage Problems

A driveway that slopes toward the house creates chronic water intrusion. Removal and regrading fixes the slope before new concrete goes in.

Driveway Removal FAQs

How much does driveway removal cost in Temecula?

Driveway removal in Temecula costs $1,800-$6,000 for most residential projects, with a standard two-car driveway running $2,500-$4,000 including breaking, hauling, and disposal.

How long does driveway removal take?

Most driveways are removed in a single working day. Larger driveways or heavily reinforced slabs may need two days.

Can you remove just part of my driveway?

Yes. We use diamond-blade saws to cut clean lines separating what's removed from what stays, common for garage expansions or damaged-section replacement.

Do I need a permit to remove my driveway?

Removal-only typically doesn't require a permit. Replacing the driveway or changing its footprint may require one — we'll flag this during the site evaluation.

Can you remove driveways with rebar?

Yes. Rebar and wire mesh require cutting, which adds labor time and factors into the quote.

Will driveway removal damage my landscaping?

We take precautions to protect existing landscaping and keep equipment on hard surfaces where possible. Minor lawn disturbance from equipment traffic is sometimes unavoidable but easily repaired.
