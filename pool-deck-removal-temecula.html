Pool deck removal is specialty work — one wrong move cracks coping, chips tile, or punctures the shell. Temecula Valley Dirt Co. handles pool deck demolition throughout Temecula, Murrieta, and Wine Country with a pool-safe process built around precision cuts and controlled breaking near the water's edge.

Pool deck removal pricing typically runs $2,500 to $6,000 depending on deck size and complexity. Call 951-517-DIRT or request a site evaluation for exact pricing.

Our Pool-Safe Process

Precision Saw Cuts

Clean cuts separate the deck from coping before any breaking begins, protecting what stays.

Hand Tools Near the Pool

Heavy equipment stays back from the pool perimeter — hand jackhammers handle the close work.

Surface Protection

Coping and tile are protected with padding during demolition to prevent chips and cracks.

Utility Awareness

We stay clear of pool equipment lines — plumbing, gas, and electrical — throughout the demo.

Pool Deck Types We Remove

Concrete Pool Decks

Standard poured or stamped concrete, the most common deck type in Temecula.

Paver Pool Decks

Brick or concrete pavers can often be salvaged for reuse if requested.

Natural Stone

Flagstone, travertine, or slate, removed carefully with the base concrete beneath.

Tile Pool Decks

Ceramic or specialty tile removed along with the mortar bed and concrete slab.
