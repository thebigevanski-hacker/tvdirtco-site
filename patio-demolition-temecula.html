A cracked, dated backyard patio is often the first thing standing between you and a pool install or a full outdoor redesign. Temecula Valley Dirt Co. handles patio demolition throughout Temecula, Murrieta, and Wine Country — breaking up concrete, hauling it away, and leaving the yard ready for what's next.

We work around pools, spas, outdoor kitchens, and landscaping daily. Equipment stays on the concrete being removed; hand tools handle the precision work near pool edges. Patio demolition pricing typically runs $1,200 to $5,000 depending on size and features to protect. Call 951-517-DIRT or request a site evaluation.

Patio Types We Remove

Pool Decks

Common when upgrading to pavers or natural stone. Equipment never approaches closer than a few feet from the pool edge — hand tools handle the rest.

Raised Patios

Elevated entertainment areas on fill or retaining walls. We address the surface and the support structure together.

Sunken Patios

Common in older homes. We break the slab and backfill the void with compacted soil to bring the grade back to yard level.

Decorative Concrete

Stamped, exposed aggregate, or colored concrete breaks and hauls the same as standard concrete.
