Built for Temecula Valley's Terrain

Temecula Valley covers a wide range of property types — vineyard and wine country parcels, equestrian and ranch properties, hillside lots with tight access, and established residential neighborhoods throughout Temecula, Murrieta, and Menifee.

Each property type changes how a project has to be approached. Hillside lots demand equipment sized for slope and access. Ranch and equestrian parcels call for equipment scaled to the acreage, not undersized for the job. Established neighborhoods often mean narrow driveways and gates that rule out full-size machinery entirely. We plan every project around the property first — matching equipment, sequencing, and access strategy to what's actually on site.

How We Work

Every job starts with a look at the site before a number gets attached to it — access, slope, gate clearance, what's already there. That's what decides the equipment, not the other way around. Tight lots get a mini excavator or compact track loader instead of machinery that won't fit through the gate. Larger acreage gets scaled up rather than under-equipped.

From there the work is sequenced — digging or clearing first, hauling built into the schedule instead of tacked on afterward, grading finished to spec instead of left rough. You get a clear answer on cost and timeline before anything starts, and updates as the job moves, not just at the end.

Common Questions

Do you serve my area?

We serve Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Wildomar, French Valley, Winchester, Lake Elsinore, Canyon Lake, and Sun City directly, with additional service throughout Southern California including Orange County and the Inland Empire.

Do I need a permit for excavation or grading work?

It depends on the scope and your city or HOA. Pool excavation, grading that affects drainage, and larger site work typically require permits; smaller dirt hauling or brush clearing jobs often don't. We'll flag early on whether your project is likely to need one, so there are no surprises mid-job.

How do I get a quote?

Call 951-517-DIRT or request a site evaluation with your address, photos, and a short description of the project. We'll follow up with next steps and scheduling.

What's included in a site evaluation?

We look at access, slope, existing conditions, and the scope of work before giving a quote, so pricing reflects the actual job instead of a guess made over the phone.
