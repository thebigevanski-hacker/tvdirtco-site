Temecula Valley Dirt Co. handles excavation throughout Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, and the rest of Riverside County — pool digs, foundation and footing prep, and utility trenching. Every job starts with a look at the site, not a number pulled from a phone call: access, slope, gate clearance, and what's already there decide the equipment, not the other way around.

Many Temecula Valley properties come with tight side yards, hillside grades, and established landscaping that rules out full-size machinery. We run compact, tight-access equipment for those jobs and scale up for larger sites — matched to the property, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Owner-led, site evaluation before the quote, and real process language instead of guesswork. Call 951-517-DIRT or request a site evaluation to get started.

How We Approach Excavation

Site Evaluation First

We assess access, slope, soil, and existing utility lines before quoting, so the number reflects the actual job.

Equipment Matched to Access

Tight side yards and hillside lots get compact, tight-access equipment. Larger sites get equipment scaled to the acreage.

Utility Marking & Protection

We coordinate utility marking before digging and protect existing lines running through the work area.

Soil Removal Included

Excavated material is hauled and disposed of as part of the job — not left in a pile for you to deal with.
