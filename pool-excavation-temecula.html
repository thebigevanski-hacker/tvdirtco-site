Pool Types We Excavate

Gunite / Shotcrete Pools

The most common pool type in Temecula Valley. Requires precise excavation with clean, straight walls and exact depth control, matched to engineering plans including slopes for drains and equipment placement.

  • • Standard rectangle and freeform shapes
  • • Infinity edge and vanishing edge pools
  • • Attached spa excavation
  • • Beach entry and tanning ledge areas

Fiberglass Pools

Fiberglass shells need excavation slightly larger than the shell dimensions for backfill and leveling. We excavate to manufacturer specs and build a level base with proper drainage around the perimeter.

  • • Precise measurements for shell fit
  • • Gravel base preparation
  • • Proper overdig for backfill access
  • • Coordination with crane placement

Vinyl Liner Pools

Vinyl liner pools use a polymer or steel wall structure. We excavate to exact dimensions with square corners and level walls, then compact the floor to prevent settling under the liner.

  • • Square corners for wall panels
  • • Level floor compaction
  • • Exact depth to match liner
  • • Smooth walls for panel installation

Our Pool Excavation Process

1

Site Assessment & Planning

We review pool plans, assess site access, identify utility lines, and plan equipment positioning — especially important for hillside lots common in Temecula Valley.

2

Utility Marking & Protection

We coordinate with Underground Service Alert (811) and mark gas, electric, water, sewer, and irrigation lines before digging begins.

3

Precision Excavation

Laser levels and GPS technology keep depth, shape, and grade on spec per engineering plans, whether that means clean gunite walls or a precise fiberglass shell fit.

4

Soil Removal & Cleanup

We haul away all excavated soil — typically 50-150 cubic yards depending on pool size — and coordinate with your pool contractor for the next steps.

Pool Excavation FAQs

How much does pool excavation cost in Temecula?

Pool excavation typically costs $2,500-$5,000 depending on pool size, site access, and soil conditions. We provide a quote after seeing the property.

How long does pool excavation take?

Most residential pools take 1-2 days. Small pools under 400 sq ft typically take 1 day; larger pools or difficult sites may take 2-3 days.

Can you excavate pools on hillside properties?

Yes — hillside pool excavation is a regular part of the work here. Compact equipment handles tight-access and sloped lots in neighborhoods like Harveston, Redhawk, and Wolf Creek.

Do you work with pool contractors?

Yes. We follow engineering specifications exactly and coordinate scheduling, utility marking, and debris removal for a smooth handoff to your pool builder.

What's included in pool excavation service?

Digging to exact depth and shape per plans, grading and leveling the pool floor, hauling away excavated soil, utility protection, and site cleanup.
