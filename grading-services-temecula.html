Grading is where a project either sets up clean or causes problems for years. We level lots, correct drainage, and prep building pads throughout Temecula Valley, working from a laser-guided read of the site rather than a guess at the slope. Whether you're building a new home, installing a pool, fixing a yard that floods, or prepping land for landscaping, the grade has to be right before anything else goes in.

We size the machine to the access and sequence the work around drainage first, so water moves away from structures instead of toward them. Call 951-517-DIRT or request a site evaluation to get started.

Grading Services We Provide

Lot Leveling

Level uneven yards for landscaping, pools, patios, or construction. Laser-guided precision keeps elevations and slopes on spec.

Drainage Correction

Fix flooding, ponding, and erosion with proper slope and drainage design — French drains, swales, and catch basins included.

Site Preparation

Prep lots for new construction, pool installation, shop buildings, ADUs, or major landscaping projects.

Slope Stabilization

Repair eroded slopes, create stable terraces, and prevent future erosion on hillside properties.

How We Approach Grading

Drainage First

We establish where water needs to go before we touch the grade, so the finished slope actually solves the problem.

Laser-Guided Precision

Elevations and slopes are set with laser-guided equipment, not eyeballed — important for pools, pads, and drainage work.

Right Equipment for Access

Tight residential lots get compact equipment; larger sites get equipment scaled to the acreage.

Clean Finish

Site left graded to spec and ready for the next phase — landscaping, pour, or construction.
