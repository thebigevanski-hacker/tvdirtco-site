How We Approach Concrete Removal

Every concrete removal job comes down to three things: breaking it efficiently, loading and hauling it without extra trips, and leaving the site clean. We plan around each of those before work starts.

Right-Sized Breaking

Standard residential slabs break down efficiently with the right equipment. Heavily reinforced or thicker commercial-grade concrete takes more time and factors into the quote.

Efficient Loading & Hauling

We load and haul in bulk rather than by hand where possible, which keeps trips to the recycling facility to a minimum and the job moving.

Precision Cutting

For partial slab removal or work around existing structures, clean cuts prevent cracking in adjacent concrete that's staying in place.

Full Recycling

Every load goes to a licensed recycling facility, not a landfill. Disposal is included in the quote — no surprise dump fees.

Our Concrete Demolition Process

1

Free On-Site Quote & Assessment

We visit your property, measure the concrete, assess thickness and reinforcement, check site access, and identify obstacles. Written quote provided same-day or within 24 hours. Call 951-517-DIRT to schedule.

2

Site Preparation & Protection

Before breaking begins, we mark underground utilities (811 DigAlert), protect adjacent landscaping, and set up staging areas for debris.

3

Breaking & Demolition

Concrete is broken into manageable sections. Reinforced concrete is cut as needed. Precision hand tools handle tight spaces near structures.

4

Loading & Hauling

Broken concrete is loaded and hauled in bulk loads to minimize trips. A standard driveway typically requires a few loads depending on thickness.

5

Recycling

All concrete goes to licensed recycling facilities in Riverside County, crushed into aggregate for road base and new construction. Disposal fees are included in the quote.

6

Final Grading & Cleanup

We rake and level the exposed dirt, clear remaining debris, and grade the area flat. Final walk-through before we leave.

What Affects Your Quote

  • Square footage — larger jobs see better price-per-sq-ft economics
  • Thickness — 4" residential vs. 8" commercial-grade affects labor and disposal weight
  • Reinforcement — wire mesh breaks easily; heavy rebar requires cutting
  • Access — equipment-accessible jobs run faster and cheaper than hand-labor-only jobs
  • Base removal — if the gravel base under the concrete also needs removal, that adds to the quote

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does concrete removal cost in Temecula?

Concrete removal in Temecula typically runs $2-$6 per square foot depending on thickness, reinforcement, access, and project size. Standard residential driveways run $2,500-$4,000 including breaking, loading, hauling, and disposal. Smaller projects start around $800. We provide free written quotes after seeing your specific project.

How long does concrete removal take?

Most projects take 1-2 days. A standard driveway is typically finished in one working day including breaking, loading, hauling, and cleanup. Larger or heavily reinforced projects may need 2 days.

Do I need a permit for concrete removal in Temecula?

Removal-only projects typically don't require permits. If you're replacing the concrete, expanding the footprint, or affecting drainage, Temecula Building & Safety may require permits. We can help you understand what your project needs.

What do you do with the broken concrete?

All concrete is hauled to licensed recycling facilities in Riverside County, crushed into aggregate for road base and new construction. Disposal fees are included in our quotes.

Can you remove concrete with rebar?

Yes, we handle reinforced concrete including rebar and wire mesh. Reinforcement adds complexity and time since it requires cutting, which factors into pricing.

Will concrete removal damage my landscaping?

We take precautions to protect existing landscaping and stay on hard surfaces when possible. Minor lawn disturbance from equipment traffic is sometimes unavoidable but easily repaired.

Can you remove concrete in tight spaces?

Yes, we handle tight-access removal throughout Temecula. For areas that don't fit larger equipment, we use hand tools and smaller machines. Tight access adds labor time and typically costs more per square foot.
