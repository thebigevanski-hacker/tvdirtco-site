Our Story

Built on Local Grit

When you hire someone to move earth on your property, you're trusting them with more than a piece of equipment. You're trusting them around your home, your landscaping, your driveway, your fences, your animals, and a project you've probably put a lot of time and money into.

Temecula Valley Dirt Co. is a locally operated earthwork and land services company serving Temecula Valley and Southwest Riverside County. We were founded in 2022 by lifelong Temecula residents who saw a need for reliable, professional excavation and land services in a growing community.

What started with a single mini excavator has grown into a full service earthwork operation serving homeowners, horse properties, ranches, vineyards, contractors, and commercial projects. But the philosophy hasn't changed: treat every property like it's our own, communicate clearly, show up when we say we will, and do the work right.

We're also longtime horse owners, so we understand that a horse property has to work for both the people and the animals living on it. The right grading, drainage, access, footing, turnout areas, shade, fencing, and overall layout can make everyday life dramatically easier. We know what it takes to improve a property while keeping the practical realities of owning horses in mind.

Every property brings its own challenges. Tight backyard access, steep terrain, hard soil, rock and boulders, drainage concerns, and limited places to put excavated material can all change how a project needs to be approached. We look beyond simply moving dirt and consider the entire site, from access and equipment selection to grading , hauling , and land clearing .

For homeowners planning a major property project, we also handle excavation services in Temecula , including the digging, material handling, access planning, and site work that can make a project go smoothly from the beginning.

We Move the Earth. And we take pride in doing it the right way.

Why Choose Us?

Local Expertise

We know Temecula Valley's terrain, soil conditions, property layouts, and the practical challenges that come with working throughout the area. This is experience earned on local job sites.

Horse & Ranch Experience

We're longtime horse owners ourselves. We understand how grading, drainage, access, footing, turnout areas, and property layout affect everyday life on a horse property.

Professional Equipment

We use the right equipment for the site and maintain it to a high standard. Equipment selection matters, especially when access is tight or the terrain gets difficult.

Tight-Access Capability

Many Temecula area properties have narrow side yards, gates, slopes, and other access limitations. Compact equipment and careful planning can make challenging spaces workable.

Clear Communication

We provide clear project scopes and communicate when site conditions change. You should know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what to expect before the equipment starts moving.

Respect Your Property

Your property isn't a blank job site. We work around existing landscaping, hardscaping, irrigation, fencing, structures, and other improvements with care. Protecting what stays is just as important as moving what goes.
